The main takeaway

The median adjusted value of the eight closed sales is $1,050,500. Your contract price of $980,000 is $70,500 below that median. That is an encouraging position, especially since this home has some dated finishes and backs toward Easter and Shawnee. Both could influence how certain buyers perceive the home in the future. The seller credit offers an additional $26,500 in transaction benefit, bringing the price-less-credit comparison to $953,500, although the recorded purchase price remains $980,000.

I am pleased that we are buying below the adjusted median rather than paying for a premium golf-course setting or a completely remodeled interior that this home does not have. That distinction is an important part of the value argument.

What an adjusted value means

Every sold property has advantages and disadvantages compared with Easter Circle. I start with what each home actually sold for, account for seller concessions, and then estimate the dollar impact of meaningful differences. The adjusted value is my opinion of what that particular sale suggests 22197 E Easter Circle should be worth if the properties were made comparable.

Understanding the road location and future resale

Easter Circle has a much larger lot than these comparisons, substantial finished living space, and a finished basement. Its setting also places it near Easter and Shawnee. Some buyers are especially sensitive to roads behind or alongside a home, while others may not view that as a major concern. It does not mean the home is necessarily less enjoyable to live in; it simply may narrow the buyer pool or affect price expectations when it is resold.

That is why buying below the adjusted median matters here. We are not paying the same price as though this home had a golf-course view or high-end, move-in-ready updates. This provides a more defensible starting point today, although future appreciation, condition, and resale results can never be guaranteed.

Conclusion

Considering the closed-sale comparisons as a group, I believe the $980,000 contract price is well supported as a reasonable purchase. Several relevant adjusted sales are near or above the agreed price, including Costilla at $990,000 and Sicily at $1,035,000. The overall median of $1,050,500 is higher still. We are entering the purchase at a level that recognizes this home's road proximity and opportunities for cosmetic improvement, with a meaningful seller credit improving the overall purchase terms.
